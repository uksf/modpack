# Radios

Configures radio channels, vehicle racks and intercom, and vehicle rack default channels.

## Radio presets

A radio preset stores the channel, volume, ear and PTT key of each radio the player carries.

- ACE Arsenal loadouts save the player's current radio settings. Loading a loadout applies its settings when the arsenal closes, after ACRE assigns the radios.
- The Radio Presets button in the ACE Arsenal, and Self Interaction > Radios > Radio Presets, open the preset manager. It saves, loads and deletes named presets in the player profile.
- A preset matches radios by type in carry order. A radio the preset does not hold keeps its settings.
